1. Objectives and Methodology of Project Proposal (PO1) [Max Marks: 5]
1.1 Problem Formulation & Grassroots Realities

In rural agricultural belts of northern India (specifically surveyed across District Gonda, Uttar Pradesh), agrarian micro-commerce operates under severe structural inefficiencies that adversely impact smallholder farmers and verified retail merchants:

  • Blind Physical Stock Hunting: Farmers travel 15–30 km across fragmented rural markets during sowing windows, frequently finding subsidized fertilizers (Urea, DAP) out of stock.
  • Economic & Productive Labor Loss: Each unconfirmed trip consumes 4–6 productive farming hours and Rs. 150–350 in deadhead transportation fuel.
  • Asymmetric Information & Middleman Markups: Lack of unified catalog visibility allows middlemen to charge premiums above the Government Maximum Sale Price (MSP) ceiling (e.g. Urea capped at Rs. 266.50/45kg sold for Rs. 320–350).
  • Paper-Based Bahi-Khata Ledger Disputes: Over 80% of local agri-inputs rely on informal seasonal credit ("Udhaar"). Traditional paper notebooks suffer from weathering, alterations, calculation errors, and contentious disputes.
1.2 Core Technological Objectives
  • Sub-100ms Hyper-Local Stock Visibility: In-memory spatial index resolving vendor catalog queries, verified stock, and geodetic distance in under 100ms within a 5km radius.
  • Tamper-Proof Digital Credit Ledger Engine: Double-entry mathematical ledger enforcing credit balance invariance, dynamic credit ceiling checks (Rs. 10,000 pool), and SHA-256 audit hashes.
  • Transparent & Verified Price Discovery: Real-time price monitoring cross-referenced against District Agriculture Officer (DAO) MSP ceilings and the National SeedTrace Portal (seedtrace.gov.in).
  • Click-to-Reserve & Central Escrow Fulfillment: Click-to-reserve workflow with atomic inventory locking, preventing double-allocation and enabling two-stage verification via dynamic 4-digit OTPs.

2.2 Mathematical Formulations & Engineering Equations
1. Bahi-Khata Running Balance Equation:
B_current = B_prev + Sum(P_j * Q_j) - R_repay
Constraint: Approved iff (B_current <= L_sanctioned), where L_sanctioned = Rs. 10,000.00
2. Thread-Safe Inventory Decrement Formula:
S_available = S_total - (S_reserved + S_confirmed)
Update Rule: Delta(S_available) = -Q_order iff (S_available >= Q_order) within atomic transaction.
3. Haversine Proximity Geofencing Equation:
a = sin²(Delta_lat / 2) + cos(lat1) * cos(lat2) * sin²(Delta_lon / 2)
d = 2 * R * arcsin(min(1, sqrt(a)))   [R = 6,371.0 km, Radius <= 5.0 km]

6. Communication (Presentation) (PO10) [Max Marks: 5]
6.1 Core Defense Viva Q&A

Q1: How does your architecture handle concurrency conflicts when two farmers purchase the last remaining bag of Urea?
Defense: We utilize pessimistic row-level locking via SQLAlchemy's .with_for_update() directive (emitting SQL SELECT ... FOR UPDATE). When Thread A requests the record, an exclusive lock is granted. Thread B is placed in a blocked state. Thread A decrements stock from 1 to 0 and commits, releasing the lock. Thread B then unblocks, reads stock as 0, fails the constraint, and receives an HTTP 409 'INSUFFICIENT_STOCK' response with zero negative stock drift.

Q2: If mobile connectivity drops immediately after a UPI payment is deducted, how is the order recovered?
Defense: Payment capture does not rely on client-side callbacks. We implement asynchronous server-to-server webhooks via Razorpay. When the payment processes, Razorpay posts directly to /api/payment/razorpay-webhook. The backend verifies the HMAC SHA-256 signature, transitions order status to ESCROW_HOLD, and issues the 4-digit OTP. When the farmer regains connectivity, querying /api/orders immediately displays the active escrow voucher and OTP.

Q3: What safeguards prevent a vendor from manually altering a farmer's Bahi-Khata debt balance in the database?
Defense: The bahi_khata_ledger is an append-only ledger; direct UPDATE or DELETE operations are prohibited at the ORM layer. Every ledger row computes an internal SHA-256 hash linking the prior record's hash, farmer ID, debit amount, and timestamp: H_k = SHA256(H_{k-1} || FarmerID || Delta || Timestamp). Any manual database tampering breaks the cryptographic hash continuity, causing the administrative telemetry audit engine to immediately flag the discrepancy.
